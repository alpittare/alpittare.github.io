Refund Policy

Last updated: June 6, 2026

Honest Debt Pro subscriptions are purchased through the Apple App Store, and all payments and refunds are processed by Apple — EXAFABS never receives or stores your payment details and cannot issue App Store refunds directly.

1. Requesting a Refund

  • Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple ID
  • Find your Honest Debt Pro purchase and choose "Request a refund"
  • Apple reviews and decides refund requests according to its own policies, typically within 24–48 hours

2. Cancelling Your Subscription

Cancelling stops future charges but does not refund past ones. To cancel: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Honest Debt Pro → Cancel. Your Pro access continues until the end of the paid period.

3. Billing Issues

If you believe you were charged in error (for example, a duplicate charge), contact us at alpit@exafabs.ai — we'll help you resolve it with Apple, and where the issue is on our side (such as an entitlement not unlocking after purchase), we'll fix it promptly.
