About this bibliography

FastAI Health Coach uses an AI assistant (powered by Anthropic's Claude) to answer fasting and nutrition questions. To ensure those responses are grounded in real research and not invented, we restrict the Coach to citing only the sources below — peer-reviewed publications by recognized researchers in intermittent fasting, metabolic health, and nutrition science.

When you ask the Coach a question and it makes a specific claim about autophagy, insulin sensitivity, ketogenesis, or any other physiological topic, you'll see an inline citation in this format: [Source: AuthorLastName Year, Journal]. You can find the full reference here.

Important: FastAI provides general wellness information based on this published research. It is not medical advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider before making medical decisions, especially if you have any medical conditions, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or take prescription medications.

How citations work in the FastAI Coach

When you chat with FastAI Coach and ask about a specific physiological mechanism — for example, "What happens during autophagy?" or "Does fasting really improve insulin sensitivity?" — the Coach will respond with a citation in the format [Source: AuthorLastName Year, Journal].

Each cited source appears in the bibliography above with a direct link to PubMed or the publishing journal so you can verify the original research yourself. We update this bibliography quarterly as new peer-reviewed research is published.

If the Coach cannot find a credible source for a specific claim, it will give you general guidance without specific numbers or mechanisms — and recommend you consult a qualified healthcare provider for individualized advice.

Important medical disclaimer

The information provided by FastAI Coach and the research summarized in this bibliography is for educational and general wellness purposes only. It is not medical advice and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.

You should always consult a qualified healthcare provider before:

  • Starting any fasting or dietary regimen, especially if you have any medical condition
  • Making changes to medications or supplements
  • Engaging in extended fasts (more than 24 hours)
  • Combining fasting with exercise programs if you have cardiovascular concerns

Fasting may not be appropriate for individuals who are pregnant or breastfeeding, have a history of eating disorders, are underweight, take prescription medications (especially diabetes medications), or have certain medical conditions.
