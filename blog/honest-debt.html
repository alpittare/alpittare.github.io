Honest Debt: AI Payoff Manager

Your debt-free date, in minutes — with no bank logins, no money movement, and no hype. Just your real numbers, computed honestly.

What it does

You type in your debts — balance, interest rate, minimum payment. Honest Debt's payoff engine instantly computes your debt-free date, total interest, interest saved, and a month-by-month journey to zero. Rough numbers are fine. Bank connections don't exist in this app, by design.

Three honest strategies

  • Avalanche — highest interest first. Saves the most money over time.
  • Snowball — smallest balance first. Quick wins build momentum.
  • Lower Bills — largest monthly payment first. Frees up your budget fastest.

Radically honest by design

We show you every path — including the ones we make no money on.

The Explore tab compares self-payoff against balance transfers, consolidation loans, debt management plans, settlement, and bankruptcy — with calm risk badges and honest costs for each. Got a loan offer? The Offer Checker tells you plainly whether it beats your current plan, or doesn't.

What the AI does (and doesn't do)

Every figure in Honest Debt is computed by a deterministic engine. The AI's only job is to explain your plan in plain English — it never invents numbers, never makes promises, and the app never gives financial advice. Estimates only; for debt counselling we point you to licensed nonprofit counselors.

Scenario Simulator

Test a change instantly: "What if I add £500 a month?" See your new debt-free date, interest saved, and a projected-balance graph — total or loan by loan — before you commit a single pound, dollar, or rupee.
